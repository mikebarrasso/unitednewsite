<p><strong>Knowing how an advisor is compensated matters when choosing financial guidance.</strong></p>

<h2>Why Fee-Only Advice Matters</h2>

<p>A fee-only advisor is compensated directly by the client, rather than through commissions from investment or insurance products. That structure can make the advisor's compensation easier to understand and can help reduce incentives tied to a particular product.</p>

<p>For households coordinating retirement, investments, and taxes, the questions often extend beyond one recommendation. A fee-only fiduciary relationship is designed to keep the conversation centered on your goals, circumstances, and the trade-offs involved. Fees, services, and the scope of advice should still be reviewed carefully before engaging any advisor.</p>
